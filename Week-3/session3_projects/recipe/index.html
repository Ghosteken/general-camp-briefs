Pepperoni Pizza

A classic homemade pizza with a crisp, golden crust, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and savory pepperoni slices.

Preparation Time

  • Prep time: 20 minutes
  • Resting time: 1 hour
  • Cook time: 12–15 minutes
  • Total time: About 1 hour 35 minutes
  • Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 30–40 pepperoni slices
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • All-purpose flour, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Let the pizza dough rest at room temperature for about 1 hour.
  2. Preheat the oven to 245°C (475°F). Place a baking tray or pizza stone inside.
  3. Dust a work surface with flour and stretch the dough into a round shape.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Arrange the pepperoni slices on top.
  7. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with oregano and garlic powder.
  8. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  9. Allow the pizza to cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
