# Session 1 – Orientation & Think Like an Engineer

**Week:** 1 – Think Like an Engineer

**Duration:** Approximately 90 minutes (Monday afternoon session)

**Theme:** Inspire curiosity, introduce the programme, and help students begin thinking like software engineers.

---

# Session Objective

By the end of this session, students should:

* Feel welcomed and excited about the programme.
* Understand what they will be building over the next four weeks.
* Understand that software engineering is about solving problems.
* Meet their instructor and begin building trust.
* Learn how websites are created.
* Begin thinking about a project they would like to build.
* Leave looking forward to the next session.

---

# Success Criteria

The session is successful if students can:

* Introduce themselves confidently.
* Explain what they would like to build.
* Identify HTML, CSS and JavaScript as the technologies they will learn.
* Explain that software is created to solve problems.
* Say they are excited about the next class.

# Session Flow

---

## Part 1 – Welcome (10 minutes)

### Introduce Yourself

Tell students:

* Your name
* Your background
* Why you enjoy technology
* Why you enjoy teaching

Show genuine enthusiasm.

Example:

> "Technology changed my life. Today I get to teach coding and robotics, and I'm excited to build amazing things together with you over the next few weeks."

---

## Part 2 – Student Icebreaker (15 minutes)

Each student introduces themselves.

Questions:

* What's your name?
* Where are you joining from?
* What's your favourite game or app?
* What's your favourite subject?
* If you could invent anything, what would it be?

Listen carefully.

Write down every project idea they mention.

These ideas will guide their portfolio and later projects.

---

## Part 3 – Robotics Demonstration (20 minutes)

This is your opportunity to make a memorable first impression.

Show a robotics project that you have built.

Possible demonstrations:

* Arduino traffic light
* Smart home project
* Ultrasonic distance sensor
* Automatic water tank controller
* Robot car
* LED animation

While demonstrating, ask:

"What do you think is happening here?"

"How do you think the computer knows what to do?"

"What would happen if I changed the program?"

Explain that software controls hardware.

Then connect that idea to websites:

> "Whether we're programming a robot or building a website, we're giving computers instructions."

---

## Part 4 – What Is Software Engineering? (10 minutes)

Explain simply.

Avoid technical language.

Suggested explanation:

Software engineering is the process of solving problems using technology.

Software engineers create things people use every day:

* YouTube
* Spotify
* WhatsApp
* Netflix
* TikTok
* Games
* Banking apps

Ask students:

"What apps do you use every day?"

Discuss briefly.

---

## Part 5 – How Websites Work (15 minutes)

Open three different websites.

Examples:

* Google
* YouTube
* BBC News

Ask:

"What do these websites have in common?"

Explain:

Every website is built using files.

Introduce:

HTML → Structure

CSS → Appearance

JavaScript → Interaction

Use a house analogy:

HTML = Bricks and walls

CSS = Paint and decoration

JavaScript = Electricity that makes everything work

Avoid going into code today.

Today's goal is understanding.

---

## Part 6 – Dream Project Activity (15 minutes)

Ask each student:

"If you could build any website or app during this programme, what would it be?"

Examples:

* Football website
* Recipe app
* School helper
* Robotics portfolio
* Personal website
* Game review site

Record their ideas.

Explain:

Every lesson we learn will help build toward that project.

---

## Part 7 – Wrap Up (5 minutes)

Review:

Today we learned:

* What software engineering is
* How websites work
* What we'll build
* Why programming is exciting

Encourage students.

Tell them:

"Next session we'll start building your own website."

---

# Homework

Find three websites that you like.

For each website answer:

1. Why do you like it?

2. What colours do you notice?

3. What would you improve?

Bring your answers to the next session.

---

# Questions to Ask During the Session

* What technology excites you the most?
* What would you love to build?
* What problem would you like technology to solve?
* What website do you visit most often?
* Why do people create websites?

---

# Teaching Tips

* Smile and keep the atmosphere relaxed.
* Keep explanations short.
* Ask questions often.
* Celebrate every contribution.
* Avoid long lectures.
* Encourage curiosity.
* Learn students' names quickly.

Remember:

Students may forget what you teach.

They rarely forget how you made them feel.

---

# AI Usage

Week 1 Rule:

**AI Explains**

Students may use AI to:

* Explain unfamiliar terms.
* Explain concepts.
* Explain errors.

Students should not ask AI to build their project for them.
